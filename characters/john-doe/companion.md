# COMPANION — INNER DEMON

In the Earth Invasion world, the companion system manifests as an Inner Demon.
The Inner Demon and the Companion are the same feature — "demon" is simply what this world calls it.

Status: Dormant (manifests on first death)
Type: Inner Demon
Influence: 0 / 100
Relationship: Not yet formed

## SEEDS (what it will be made of when it wakes)
- The footballer who destroyed his body chasing something he couldn't reach
- The boy who bullied his disabled best friend — was one of the worst
- The son who blamed his mother for poverty she couldn't control
- The grandson who tripped an old woman and laughed, then saw her face
- The depression hidden behind a wide grin
- The emptiness of a life built for a purpose he cannot name

## HOW IT MANIFESTS
At moment of first death, the System performs an emergency resonance extraction — pulling a compressed echo of the player's deepest fears, failures, and suppressed self-knowledge into a semi-autonomous entity within the mental architecture.

## RELATIONSHIP STAGES
1. Dormant — pre-first death. No companion. No awareness.
2. Hostile — post-death, low integration. Mocks, provokes, tests.
3. Negotiated — player has acknowledged it. Tension becomes complex.
4. Integrated — player has accepted the aspects it represents. Becomes an asset.
5. Dominant (failure) — player loses control. Demon runs the body for 24h.

## NOTES
Will not be a simple monster. Knows the difference between stubbornness and self-destruction — and knows he can't tell them apart either.
