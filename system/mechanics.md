# GAME MECHANICS

## CORE SYSTEMS

- Leveling
- Skills
- Inventory
- Factions
- World ranking

---

## CURSE: TIME REWIND

- On death → rewind 24 hours
- Player remembers everything
- Pain is fully experienced

---

## MENTAL STATE

- Scale: 0-100

Effects:
- 0-30 → stable
- 30-60 → stress (small randomness)
- 60-90 → instability (actions may fail/change)
- 100 → DEMON TAKEOVER

---

## COMPANION SYSTEM (INNER DEMON)

- Exists in mental world
- Appears after first death
- Represents fears/failures
- Not purely evil

Behavior:
- Judges player
- Teaches power/authority
- Can fight player

---

## COMPANION TAKEOVER

At 100 mental strain:
- Player loses control
- Goes berserk
- Unique transformation

To recover:
- Must defeat companion/demon in mental world

---

## RANDOM FAILURE SYSTEM

At high instability:
- Actions may:
  - partially fail
  - misfire
  - change intent
