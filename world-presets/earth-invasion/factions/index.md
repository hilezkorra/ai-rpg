# FACTIONS INDEX

## Active on Earth

### Surviving Humans
Status: Scattered, leaderless. Approximately 2% of pre-invasion population (~160 million worldwide).
Goal: Survive. Some are awakening to the System. Resistance groups forming in major urban areas.
See: surviving-humans.md

### The Vorath
Status: Occupation force. Harvester ships in low orbit. Taker drones in all major urban centers.
Goal: Resource extraction, genetic/psychic assessment, access to the 98% in tutorial space.
See: vorath.md

### The Shriven
Status: Research force. Node clusters in low orbit. Active contact attempts with isolated survivors.
Goal: Understand the System. Potentially recover something they lost when they shed their bodies.
See: shriven.md

### The Pale Court
Status: Observers. Nominally non-interventionist by their own compact. Suspected defector on surface.
Goal: Observe the trial. Looking for resonance singularity. Holding the arrangement with the Vorath.
See: pale-court.md

## Unknown / Uncontacted

### Tutorial Population
Status: 98% of humanity in extradimensional stasis/processing space.
Goal: Unknown from their perspective. Vorath are accessing them. System is partially protecting them.

### Chernobyl Presence
Status: Unknown. Entity or entities that cause immediate withdrawal from both Vorath and Shriven.
Goal: Unknown. May predate the invasion.

### Terminal Zero Source
Status: Unknown. Broadcasting a signal in a language that predates the Pale Court.
Goal: Unknown. Possibly awaiting a response.
