# SURVIVING HUMANS

## Name and Ideology
No unified name. The 2% who remain. Scattered, unorganized, operating on instinct and grief.

There is no ideology yet — ideology requires communication, and communication requires surviving long enough to meet other people. Most of the 2% have never encountered another survivor. Those who have tend to either form fragile alliances or collapse into violence, depending on what the System has done to them.

## Who Are They?
The 2% were not chosen by any human institution. The Vorath classification tagged them as "low-yield anomalies" — people whose biological or psychological profile made them unsuitable for standard tutorial processing. This includes:

- Individuals with extreme neurological divergence (high resilience to System integration, unusual pattern-recognition, or psychic anomalies)
- Individuals near death at the moment of the pull — the System's selection algorithm deprioritized the already-compromised
- Individuals in heavily shielded locations (deep underground, certain industrial facilities)
- A deliberate small percentage left as "control seeds" — the Pale Court requested them

None of the 2% know which category they fall into. Some assume they were rejected. Some assume they were chosen. Both interpretations lead to problems.

## Condition Post-Invasion
- No central government. No military command. Communications are intermittent.
- Power grids are failing in most regions. The Vorath are not actively destroying infrastructure — they just stopped caring about its maintenance.
- Food supply is functionally intact in rural areas. Cities are increasingly dangerous due to Vorath Taker drones cataloging urban zones.
- Most survivors are in an acute trauma state. The System does occasionally provide UI elements, prompts, and stat displays — but for those who never went to tutorial, these appear without context, without tutorial, and without explanation.
- Internet is partially functional (the Shriven maintain it — they find human data exchange fascinating and don't want it to stop).

## Early Resistance / Notable Patterns
- Survivors who've leveled even once tend to find each other. The System seems to generate proximity notifications at early tiers.
- Small groups have formed in certain cities — usually around a single high-level survivor or someone with a natural leadership disposition.
- No organized global resistance yet. The numbers aren't there.
- The term "The Remnants" has begun appearing in System-related communications — possibly referring to the 2% collectively. Possibly self-applied. Origin unclear.

## Regional Differences
- **Berlin, Germany**: Relatively intact. Low Vorath activity. A concentration of anomalous survivors has led to mild System-assisted networking. One high-level survivor is known to be in the area.
- **Tokyo, Japan**: Heavy Shriven presence. Unusual number of survivors reporting memory anomalies and gaps.
- **São Paulo, Brazil**: Vorath Takers active in force. High survivor mortality. A pocket resistance formed around a single E-rank survivor who's demonstrated tactical competence.
- **Rural Mongolia**: Nearly untouched. The System appears to be running a concentrated lore-unlock event in this region. Unknown reason.
- **Chernobyl Exclusion Zone**: Complete Vorath and Shriven avoidance. Something there that no one has yet explained.
