# THE VORATH

## Name and Ideology
The Vorath. In their own language: "Those Who Complete the Harvest."
Ideology: The universe is a finite resource. All life, energy, and potential exists to be converted into something more efficient. Waste — an uncultivated mind, an undeveloped world — is the closest thing they have to a sin.

## Biology and Appearance
Silicon-carbon hybrid organisms. Their bodies are structured around a crystalline lattice that runs along their spine and skull, glowing faintly amber when active. Six-limbed, bilaterally symmetric but asymmetric in function — four limbs for manipulation, two for locomotion. No individual faces. They communicate via bioluminescent pattern-pulses along their thorax.

They are a hivemind, but a *layered* one. Low-caste workers share a collective thread. High-caste "Assessors" can selectively disconnect and reason individually — making them unpredictable in a way their drones are not.

Average height: 2.4 meters. Exoskeletal. Their blood runs black.

## Power System / Technology
Vorath technology is biological rather than mechanical. They grow their tools. Their ships are living organisms, fed and cultivated over centuries. Their weapons interface directly with their crystalline nervous system — a Vorath soldier doesn't carry a weapon, it *grows* one from its own body.

They have no interest in energy weapons. Their preferred method of conquest is **resource lock** — cutting off a planet's communication, suppressing organized resistance, and then systematically assessing and extracting what is valuable.

Assessment drones (called "Takers") are roughly human-sized constructs of compressed bio-material. Nearly silent. They do not fight. They *catalog*.

## Goal on Earth
Earth is a Tier 3 resource site — valuable, not critical. The Vorath want:
1. **Genetic archive** — human biology holds anomalous traits of interest (neuroplasticity, emotional amplitude, the rare emergence of what they call "resonance," which humans call psychic potential)
2. **The 98%** — the humans absorbed into tutorial space are not being educated. They are in **stasis storage**. The Vorath brokered a deal with a third party (see: The Pale Court) to access them in bulk
3. **Earth's crust** — secondary mineral interest. Would take decades to fully extract.

They do not want to destroy Earth. They want to *process* it cleanly.

## Attitude Toward Humans
Contemptuous utility. A Vorath Assessor views a human the way a botanist views a weed that turned out to be medicinally significant — mildly interesting, but nothing that inspires respect. Individual humans are irrelevant. Patterns are what matter.

The 2% who remain on Earth are anomalies by Vorath classification. Either too resistant to be cataloged, or flagged as seeds — left to test whether the species can self-correct under pressure.

## Internal Conflict
**The Cultivators** vs **The Purge Faction**

Cultivators believe the anomalous human traits (particularly resonance/psychic potential) are worth long-term investment. Some humans, given centuries, might become productive members of the Vorath supply chain — and the diversity of human cognition has potential no other harvested species has shown.

The Purge Faction thinks this is sentimental waste. Humans are unstable, irrational, and dangerously adaptive. The longer you let them run, the more expensive the eventual processing. Purge and move on.

The Vorath High Council has not resolved this debate. It's why the 2% haven't been collected yet.
