# POWER SYSTEM — SYSTEM INTEGRATION

## How Abilities Manifest
The System interfaces with human neural architecture at the quantum level — specifically, with the electromagnetic signature of consciousness. Every human has a unique "resonance profile" — a pattern of cognitive and emotional architecture that the System reads and uses as a framework.

Abilities don't come from external power. They come from the System amplifying something already present — a latent potential, a deeply held capacity, a survival instinct that was always there but never had the resolution to express itself.

A person who has spent their life reading people's emotions under stress manifests perception and social manipulation abilities. A person who survived through physical endurance manifests regenerative or strength-based abilities. A person who has always controlled their fear by thinking through worst-case scenarios manifests tactical or prediction abilities.

The System doesn't grant power. It makes visible and usable what was already there.

## Class and Specialization
Class is not chosen. It is determined by the System's analysis of your core resonance profile — essentially, the ability pattern most deeply rooted in who you are.

The **Core Memory** determines class seed: the moment of greatest emotional intensity in your life becomes the anchor for your primary specialization. This is not always the moment you'd expect, and survivors often find their class reflects a truth about themselves they were not ready to face.

Classes are not named by the System at low ranks. You discover your class through use. By D-rank, a pattern is identifiable. By B-rank, the class is fully defined.

Known class families (partial list — others may exist):
- **Striker**: Physical amplification, speed, impact
- **Anchor**: Endurance, protection, regeneration
- **Shadow**: Stealth, deception, perception
- **Resonant**: Mental manipulation, emotional amplification, psychic pressure
- **Architect**: System-adjacent abilities — construct creation, spatial manipulation, system reading
- **Unclassified (Null)**: Rare. The System cannot determine a core resonance. Usually means either extreme trauma (shattered resonance profile) or a resonance singularity candidate

## Ranking System
Rankings track System integration depth — how fully a person's consciousness has merged with the System framework.

| Rank | Name | Notes |

## Progression Logic
Ranking up requires fulfilling three conditions:

1. **Threshold accumulation**: Standard experience from combat, survival, discovery, and problem-solving. The System values *variety* — grinding the same activity yields diminishing returns.

2. **Resonance event**: At least one significant emotional event per rank — a moment that tests and expands the core resonance. Typically: confronting a deep fear, accepting a truth about yourself, or surviving something that should have ended you.

3. **Integration payment**: Rank-up is not free. The System takes something — a memory, a habit, a certainty — to make room for the new architecture. What is lost is not random. It is the thing most in conflict with who you are becoming.

Most survivors don't notice the Integration Payment until later. Some do, and resist it. Resistance creates instability.

## Limits and Costs
**The Resonance Ceiling**: Your power is bounded by your self-knowledge. You can accumulate stats indefinitely, but ability potential caps based on how deeply you understand your own resonance profile. True breakthroughs — crossing rank thresholds — require genuine self-confrontation, not just grinding.

**Mental Strain Cost**: Every active ability use draws from your mental stability. Passive abilities cost nothing. Active abilities — especially high-tier ones — can create significant mental strain spikes. Sustained use without recovery degrades the mental state permanently if not managed.

**Fractured Integration**: If mental state reaches maximum strain while abilities are active, **Fractured Integration** occurs. The resonance profile cracks. Abilities become unstable — sometimes activating involuntarily, sometimes failing entirely. Recovery from Fractured Integration requires extended rest and, often, direct confrontation with whatever caused the mental state to spike.

**Visibility**: Every rank-up increases your "resonance emission" — the System signature you broadcast. At F and E, you are essentially invisible to non-human entities tracking System signatures. By C-rank, Vorath Assessors begin flagging you for higher-level evaluation. By A-rank, you are visible from orbit.

## The Second Ability
At E-rank, the System offers one ability from a list of three. At every subsequent rank, one additional ability is available from a narrowing list — the options becoming more specific to your developing class profile.

There are rumors among survivors of a "hidden fourth option" at each ability selection — an ability that does not appear on the standard list but can be unlocked by refusing all three presented options and remaining in the selection state for an extended period. This is unverified. The one survivor who claimed to have done it described the hidden option as "something personal."
