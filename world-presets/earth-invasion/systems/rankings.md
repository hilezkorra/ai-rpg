# RANKING SYSTEM

## System Integration Ranks

| Rank | Tier Name | Description |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| F | Awakened | First contact with System. Stat windows visible. Passive buffs only. Most survivors are here. |
| E | Integrated | First active ability unlocked. System can be consciously queried. Core class starting to form. |
| D | Established | Core class identified. 2+ active abilities. Visible resonance signature detectable by System users. |
| C | Emergent | Significant power gap from lower tiers. Abilities begin compounding. Physical changes manifest. |
| B | Advanced | Tier break. B-rank and above are qualitatively different. Alien entities begin tracking you. |
| A | Sovereign | Peak human potential. Can engage Vorath drones directly. Fewer than 100 on Earth pre-invasion. |
| S | Apex | Theoretical. No confirmed human S-rank on Earth post-invasion. Pre-invasion: 3 known, now in tutorial. |
| SS | Transcendent | No confirmed human example. Pale Court mid-tier equivalent. |
| SSS | Singular | Theoretical. May correspond to resonance singularity event. |
| ??? | Redacted | Pale Court records reference a rank beyond SSS. Designation classified. |

## Known Ranking Rules
- Rank is not announced — the System tracks it but does not broadcast it to others
- Physical appearance begins subtly changing at C-rank (eye color shifts, reflexes visible, the body moves differently)
- Resonance emission increases with rank — F and E are essentially invisible; C and above are detectable to calibrated sensors; A and above are visible from orbit
- Rank cannot be reduced by normal means (combat loss, stat damage) — only by Fractured Integration events

## Rank Distribution (Day 1 Estimates)
- F-rank: ~40% of the 2% (newly awakened or not yet interacted with System)
- E-rank: ~35% (completed first integration, have an active ability)
- D-rank: ~20% (core class forming, higher capability)
- C-rank: ~4% (emerged before invasion, or extremely rapid post-invasion development)
- B-rank and above: ~1% (extremely rare, pre-invasion practitioners or exceptional post-invasion cases)

## Human Rankings Board
The System does not publish a public leaderboard for humans. However, at D-rank and above, proximity to other System users creates a passive awareness — you can sense rough power differentials. High-rank survivors often find themselves sought out by lower-rank survivors who can't explain why they feel drawn toward them.

## Alien Equivalents
- Vorath Assessors are approximately C-rank equivalent in combat
- Vorath ground combat units are approximately B-rank
- Shriven nodes do not have combat rankings in the human sense — their threat model is entirely different
- Pale Court defectors that are physically present operate outside the ranking system entirely
