# GLOBAL STATE

## World Status
Earth is under extraterrestrial occupation. Three alien factions are active. Human civilization is functionally dismantled.

**Primary situation**: 98% of humanity vanished into a System-controlled extradimensional space ("tutorial"). The 2% who remain are scattered, largely unorganized, and operating without institutional support.

**Alien presence**:
- Vorath harvester ships in low orbit over all major cities. Taker drones in urban zones.
- Shriven node clusters in low orbit, pursuing independent research agenda.
- Pale Court — unknown presence. Suspected defector active on surface.

## Factions
- Surviving Humans (scattered, ~2% of pre-invasion population)
- The Vorath (harvesters, resource-extraction focus)
- The Shriven (consciousness researchers, non-violent but dangerous)
- The Pale Court (observers, nominally non-interventionist, architects of the System)

## Power Situation
- Electrical grids: 60–80% capacity (Vorath not sabotaging)
- Communications: Intermittent (Shriven maintain internet backbone)
- Food supply: Months of reserve intact
- Government/military: Collapsed at command level. Isolated units operating independently.

## System Activity
System is active and interfacing with all surviving humans. F-rank awakenings ongoing. No confirmed E-rank or above among known Berlin-area survivors except one D-rank individual.

## Immediate Threats
- Vorath Taker drone cataloging expanding from urban cores
- Mental strain from System integration among inexperienced survivors
- Shriven "interview" contact protocols beginning in coastal European cities
- Unknown entity in Chernobyl Exclusion Zone (Vorath and Shriven both avoid)
