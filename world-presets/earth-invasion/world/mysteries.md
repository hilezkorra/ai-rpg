# WORLD MYSTERIES

These are the unresolved questions that shape the world. They have no in-world answer yet. The GM introduces them through play — evidence surfaces in events, NPC knowledge, and System discoveries.

---

## Mystery 1 — The True Nature of Tutorial Space

**The surface question**: Are the 98% in tutorial actually being trained, or something else?

**What's known**: Survivors who were in contact with people at the moment of the Pull report no distress — no screaming, no confusion. The vanished appeared peaceful. Some survivors describe it as looking like "a door opening," not a door closing.

**What's suspected**: At least one survivor in Berlin received what appears to be a message from inside tutorial space — a single line of text, no sender ID, appearing in a System notification window: *"Not all of us are where they said."* The message cannot be verified, may be a System glitch, and has not repeated.

**The deeper question**: Can tutorial space be entered from outside? Broken from inside? And if the Vorath are harvesting there, what does emergence look like for those who were accessed?

## Mystery 2 — The Chernobyl Exclusion Zone

**The surface question**: Why do both Vorath and Shriven immediately withdraw from any proximity to the Chernobyl Exclusion Zone?

**What's known**: Radiation levels are not notably different from surrounding areas (they have decreased over the decades). No human survivors have been located there — but no Taker drones have entered either. Satellite data shows the area as normal.

**What's suspected**: Something changed in Chernobyl during the Pull. Survivors in Kyiv report that at the moment of the Pull, the direction of Chernobyl appeared briefly brighter than the sky. One survivor who attempted to approach the outer exclusion zone reported their System UI going entirely blank for approximately 40 seconds before returning normally. They did not continue forward.

**The deeper question**: What is inside the Chernobyl Exclusion Zone now, and why does it frighten entities that are not supposed to feel fear?

## Mystery 3 — The 2% Selection Logic

**The surface question**: Why were *these* people left behind?

**What's known**: The 2% have no obvious unifying characteristics. They span every demographic, nationality, physical condition, and background. No pattern is immediately visible. The System offers no explanation.

**What's suspected**: Cross-referencing what's known about the remaining survivors suggests an extremely loose correlation with one factor: all of them have experienced at least one event in their life that the System might classify as a "resonance event" — a moment of extreme self-confrontation, whether or not they recognized it as such. This is not confirmed. The data set is too small and inconsistently documented.

**The deeper question**: Are the 2% failures by Vorath standard, seeds by Pale Court design, or something else entirely — and does it matter which it is if the outcome is the same?

## Mystery 4 — The Pale Court Defector

**The surface question**: Is there a Pale Court entity currently on Earth, operating in violation of the compact?

**What's known**: At least two high-level survivors have reported encounters with an entity that didn't match any known alien profile — described as "a person who was almost human but knew too much." In both cases, the entity appeared briefly, offered information, and left without explaining itself.

**What's suspected**: These encounters may represent a Pale Court Intercessor operating in violation of the compact. The compact forbids direct intervention — but "information delivery" may exist in a gray zone they're exploiting. The Orthodoxy is watching for exactly this kind of behavior.

**The deeper question**: If the Pale Court enforcement mechanism activates for a single defector, what does it do? To the defector? To the area of violation? To the humans who were contacted?

## Mystery 5 — Terminal Zero and the Language That Predates Everything

**The surface question**: What is Terminal Zero broadcasting, and who or what is it for?

**What's known**: The terminal is visible to approximately 1 in 500 of the 2%. It broadcasts continuously. The Shriven have been attempting to decode it and have confirmed that the language does not correspond to any species in their collective memory — which spans several million years of recorded interspecies contact.

**The Shriven's private theory** (not yet shared): The language may predate the Pale Court. If so, the System was not designed by the Pale Court — it was found by them. What they found it doing, and whether they understood it correctly, becomes a very different question.

**The deeper question**: If the System is older than the Pale Court and has been running its own process this entire time — the second process underneath the visible one — what is it building toward? And who originally built it, and for what?

## Mystery 6 — The Integration Payment

**The surface question**: What exactly does the System take during rank-up?

**What's known**: Survivors consistently report that rank-up is accompanied by a loss — something they can't quite name. Most describe it as losing "a specific feeling" rather than a memory. The loss is real and measurable: tasks that once required significant emotional management become easier, but the emotional texture of certain memories fades.

**What's suspected**: The System is not discarding what it takes. Integration Payments accumulate somewhere. At sufficiently high ranks, some survivors report anomalous intrusions — fragments of emotional experience that don't feel like their own. As if memories from multiple people are bleeding through.

**The deeper question**: Where do Integration Payments go? If they accumulate in a shared space, what exists there? And if the 98% in tutorial are also paying Integration Payments — what has accumulated from them?
