# WORLD TIMELINE

## Day 0 — The Event

**Hour 0 — The Silence**
All global communication ceases simultaneously. Phones, internet, radio — not jammed, simply silent. No interference signal. No explanation. Like the world held its breath.

**Hour 0 + 17 minutes — The Pull**
98% of Earth's human population vanishes. No warning, no physical violence, no visible mechanism. Witnesses describe a soft white light and then absence — the people simply completed. Cities empty in seconds. Cars stop mid-drive. Meals left mid-bite. The world continues, absent of most of the people who lived in it.

**Hour 1 — The System Activates**
The first System windows appear. Floating UI elements — stat bars, notification prompts, text in the viewer's native language — manifest in the vision of the 2% who remain. Most interpret this as hallucination or psychological shock.

**Hour 6 — First Contact**
Vorath harvester ships enter low orbit over all major cities. They do not broadcast. They do not land. They position themselves and begin passive scanning operations. The first Taker drones are deployed into urban centers.

**Hour 12 — Taker Drones Go Active**
Vorath assessment drones (Takers) begin systematic cataloging of remaining humans and resources. They do not attack. They photograph, sample, and move on. Survivors who attempt to interact with them report the drones "look through" them — as if they're objects, not entities.

**Hour 18 — The Shriven Arrive**
Three Shriven node clusters enter Earth's atmosphere, drawn by the System's activation signature. They establish orbital positions separate from the Vorath. No contact with Vorath upon arrival. Acknowledged each other's presence and continued their own operations.

**Hour 24 — End of Day 0**
Approximately 1,400 survivors worldwide have accepted the System as real and completed the F-rank awakening. Most of Earth's surface is structurally intact. The silence of cities has given way to something worse: occasional sounds, distant, that don't belong to anything human.

## Day 1 (Current)

The first day survivors have had to think clearly. Shock is wearing off into something harder. The System continues offering prompts. Food, water, and shelter remain available — barely. The first survivor-to-survivor contact events are beginning to occur in major metropolitan areas.

**Known activities today:**
- Vorath Taker drones expanding cataloging radius from city centers
- Shriven nodes making first contact attempts with isolated survivors (primarily in Tokyo and coastal European cities)
- System appears to be running an event in rural Mongolia — anomalous quest chains activating for survivors in that region
- At least one D-rank survivor confirmed in Berlin area — drawing mild Vorath Assessor attention

## Hidden Threats (Not Yet Visible)

**The Processing Window**: The Vorath's arrangement with the Pale Court has a time limit. When the window closes, Vorath access to tutorial space ends regardless of completion status. The Vorath are on a schedule. This creates urgency that may translate to aggression.

**System Partition Activity**: The System has been routing high-resonance tutorial participants into secure partitions. The Vorath know something is wrong with their access yields — they're getting less than projected. When they identify the cause, they will attempt to override it.

**The Pale Court Defector**: One Intercessor has already violated the compact and is on Earth, in human-adjacent form. They are watching a specific survivor. Their presence, if detected by the Orthodoxy, triggers the compact's enforcement mechanism — the nature of which is unknown but presumably significant.

**Terminal Zero**: The un-decodeable terminal is broadcasting with increasing intensity. Something is listening for the response. It is unclear whether that something is inside or outside the current observable reality.
