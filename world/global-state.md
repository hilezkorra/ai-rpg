# GLOBAL STATE

## World Status
Earth is under extraterrestrial occupation. Three alien factions are active. Human civilization is functionally dismantled.

**Primary situation**: 98% of humanity vanished into a System-controlled extradimensional space ("tutorial"). The 2% who remain are scattered, largely unorganized, and operating without institutional support.

**Alien presence**:
- Vorath harvester ships in low orbit over all major cities including Leipzig. Taker drones beginning urban cataloging.
- Shriven node clusters in low orbit, pursuing independent research agenda.
- Pale Court — unknown presence. Suspected defector active on surface.

## Factions
- Surviving Humans (scattered, ~2% of pre-invasion population)

- The Pale Court (observers, nominally non-interventionist, architects of the System)
