# HUMANITY POST-INVASION

## The Silence
It began without warning. At 03:17 UTC on Day 0, all global communications went dark simultaneously. Not jamming — silence. As if the signal had simply stopped caring to travel.

Seventeen minutes later, 98% of Earth's human population experienced the same thing: a white light, no sound, no sensation, and then nothing. Witnesses — the 2% who remained — describe it differently. Some say the people around them didn't disappear. They say they *completed*. Like a sentence ending. No screaming. No confusion. Just absence.

## Surviving Infrastructure
- **Electrical grids**: Functioning at 60–80% capacity in most regions. The Vorath have not actively attacked infrastructure — they need it intact for their own assessment operations. Fuel supply chains are disrupted as unmanned systems fail from neglect.
- **Internet**: Partially operational. The Shriven maintain core backbone routing — they want human data to keep flowing. Certain data centers are inaccessible (Vorath assessment zones).
- **Food supply**: Months of supply remain in distribution networks. Rural areas with farming capability are the safest long-term. Urban food stores will last weeks to months depending on population density of survivors.
- **Hospitals and emergency services**: Non-functional at institutional scale. Individual medical personnel are active in survivor groups.
- **Government and military**: Command structures collapsed when command-level personnel were taken. Junior officers and isolated units operate independently. No nation has a coherent military response.

## The System Intrusion
For the 2%, the System is present but unexplained. Those who went to tutorial presumably received onboarding. Those who remained get raw System UI — stat windows, level notifications, skill prompts — with no context. Many survivors initially believed they were hallucinating from trauma.

By Day 1, approximately 30% of the 2% have accepted the System as real and begun attempting to interact with it. The learning curve is steep and dangerous. Several deaths have been attributed to survivors attempting to activate offensive skills without understanding cooldowns or mental strain costs.

## Known Resistance Nodes
- **Berlin Cell**: Unstructured. Multiple survivors concentrated in Mitte and Prenzlauer Berg. One survivor has reportedly reached D-rank. Others unknown.
- **São Paulo Pocket**: Organized around a single tactical leader. Has made contact with Vorath drone units and survived. Significant System integration.
- **Unnamed Mongolian Collective**: Isolated. No contact established. System activity anomalously high in their region.

## What Survivors Know
Almost nothing with certainty. Most functional survivor knowledge:
1. The people who vanished are not confirmed dead (absence ≠ death)
2. The floating UI and stat windows are real and consistent across survivors
3. Some aliens kill on sight; some do not; most ignore you if you're below a certain level
4. The stronger you get, the more attention you attract

What survivors believe that may be wrong:
1. That the tutorial is a preparation program (it is not only that)
2. That the aliens are a monolithic force (there are at least three distinct factions with separate agendas)
3. That getting strong enough will solve the problem (strength is necessary but not sufficient)
